Fight Card

UFC 297 was held on January 20, 2024, in Toronto and featured two title fights: Sean Strickland vs. Dricus du Plessis for the Middleweight Championship and Raquel Pennington vs. Mayra Bueno Silva for the vacant Women’s Bantamweight title.

Location

UFC 297 was held at the Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, on January 20, 2024. UFC 297 was held in Canada because Toronto is one of the UFC’s strongest and most profitable markets, and the promotion intentionally returned after a six‑year absence to capitalize on fan demand and new Canadian broadcast partnerships.
