Info About the UFC

The UFC, or Ultimate Fighting Championship, is the largest mixed martial arts (MMA) organization in the world. It was founded in 1993 and features fighters who compete using a combination of martial arts styles, including boxing, wrestling, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, kickboxing, and Muay Thai. UFC fights take place inside an eight-sided cage called the Octagon, where athletes compete under specific rules and weight classes. The UFC has grown from a small combat-sports event into a major global sports organization, with events held in countries around the world and millions of fans watching each year. Fighters compete to win matches, climb the rankings, and eventually challenge for a UFC championship title.

The Style of Fighting

The UFC features many different types of fighting because it is a form of mixed martial arts, or MMA. Fighters can use striking techniques such as boxing, kickboxing, and Muay Thai, which include punches, kicks, knees, and elbows. They can also use wrestling to take an opponent to the ground and control their position. Brazilian jiu-jitsu is another important style, focusing on grappling, submissions, and techniques that can force an opponent to give up. Fighters often combine several of these styles during a match, making UFC fights a mix of striking, wrestling, and ground fighting.

How the Weight Classes Work

Weight classes in the UFC are used to make fights as fair and safe as possible by matching fighters with opponents who are around the same weight. Fighters must weigh in before their fights and stay within the limit for their division. The men’s weight classes are flyweight (125 pounds), bantamweight (135 pounds), featherweight (145 pounds), lightweight (155 pounds), welterweight (170 pounds), middleweight (185 pounds), light heavyweight (205 pounds), and heavyweight (265 pounds). The UFC also has women's divisions, including strawweight (115 pounds), flyweight (125 pounds), and bantamweight (135 pounds). Championship fights generally require fighters to meet the exact weight limit, while non-title fights usually allow a one-pound allowance. By separating fighters into weight classes, the UFC helps reduce major differences in size and strength between opponents.
