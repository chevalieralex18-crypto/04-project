Fight Card

The next fight card will be UFC 333 featuring Alex Volkanovski versus Movsar Evloev as the main event and Petr Yan versus Merab Dvalishvili as the co-main event. This UFC card will air on Paramount+ at 10:00 AM ET on October 24, 2026.

Location

UFC 333 is being held in Abu Dhabi because the city has become an important international destination for UFC events. The UFC has had a long partnership with Abu Dhabi that began in 2010, and the city has hosted more than 20 UFC events.
